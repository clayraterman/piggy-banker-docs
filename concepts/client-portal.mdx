# Create and enter a client workspace

A client workspace is the security, data, and operating boundary for one business. Its connected accounts, Context, metrics, values, Pages, artifacts, and agent runs must never resolve against another client.

## Before you create it

Collect:

- the legal and operating name,
- the primary entity and any additional entities,
- fiscal year and reporting conventions,
- the responsible CFO team members,
- the client collaborators who need access, and
- the accounting system that will become the required ledger source.

## Setup sequence

1. Create the client under the correct firm.
2. Grant only the access needed for setup.
3. Enter the client's [business Context](/concepts/context).
4. Connect and verify the [general ledger](/setup/connect-general-ledger).
5. Add optional bank/card sources.
6. Review source readiness and freshness.
7. Create the first metrics and models.

## Multi-entity clients

A client may authorize more than one accounting realm or tenant. Keep each connection independent so its entity and lineage remain clear. Do not merge or sum connected sources simply because they belong to the same client.

When multiple sources can answer the same finance domain, Piggy Banker selects one ready owner for that domain. Other sources remain available for reconciliation or fallback; they are not automatically aggregated.

## Workspace versus portal access

Internal firm members work in the client workspace. Client collaborators use authenticated, client-scoped access appropriate to their role. The portal remains a separate shell so internal controls cannot leak through a hidden navigation treatment.

<Warning>
  Never reuse one client's metric identifier, source reference, artifact link, or agent destination for another client. Piggy Banker rejects cross-client references, but names and descriptions should still make the intended scope obvious to operators.
</Warning>
