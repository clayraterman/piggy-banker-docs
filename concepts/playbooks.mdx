# Templates and Playbooks

Templates and Playbooks are the firm-level library for reusing good finance work across clients. They sit outside the five-destination client navigation.

<Note>
  **Availability:** Versioned agent templates exist today. The unified firm-owned library for Page templates and operating Playbooks is coming soon.
</Note>

## Keep three concepts distinct

### Agent template

A reusable workflow blueprint. Publishing creates an immutable revision; a client deployment chooses a revision, schedule, destinations, Context, approval policy, and overrides.

### Page template

A reusable layout for a model or dashboard. It should contain structure, block settings, and semantic metric bindings—not copied client values.

### Operating Playbook

A repeatable finance process: purpose, cadence, inputs, procedures, review steps, output format, owners, and control points. A Playbook may refer to Page templates and agent templates without collapsing them into one object.

## What reuse should preserve

- the business question,
- layout and reporting grammar,
- metric intent,
- required source categories,
- review and approval steps,
- expected output format, and
- version history.

## What reuse must not copy

- another client's values,
- connected-account identifiers,
- private Context,
- destination credentials or channels,
- client-specific assumptions, or
- permissions.

## Resolve per client

When applying a template, Piggy Banker should resolve its required metrics and sources inside the target client. Missing dependencies become setup work; they must not fall back to another client's objects.

## Version deliberately

Treat published operating knowledge as immutable. Create a new revision for material changes, test it with a client deployment, and keep run history tied to the exact version that produced the output.
