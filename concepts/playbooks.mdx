Reuse a useful Page layout, operating procedure, or agent workflow without treating another client's numbers as your own.

## Three different resources

| Resource | What it preserves | Where it is used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page template | Page structure and reusable metric bindings | Apply inside a company or managed-client workspace |
| Playbook | Written procedures, policies, and guidance for interpreting the numbers | Pages and authorized agent context |
| Agent template | Workflow instructions, configuration, and published revisions | Client agent deployments |

Page templates and playbooks live on the customer account's **Templates & playbooks** shelf. Company accounts own their resources; CFO Firms can reuse theirs across managed clients. The shelf is available from the sidebar, including a read-only browse experience for a firm with no clients yet.

## Save a Page to the library

1. Open a Page with the content you want to reuse.
2. Open **Share**, then **Save as template…**, or choose **Export** → **Save…**.
3. Choose **Template** or **Playbook** and give it a clear name.
4. Review the reusable content before saving. An empty Page cannot be saved as a useful template.

Metric references are rebound to the target client where they can be resolved. Unresolved or ambiguous bindings require review; they must not silently point back to another client's metric.

<Warning>
Reusable prose and manually entered examples can still contain sensitive information. Remove client names, private notes, pasted values, and confidential assumptions before saving content for account-wide reuse. Safe metric rebinding does not sanitize your writing.
</Warning>

## Apply and verify

Open the shelf in the target client, choose the entry, and inspect the resulting Page. Confirm each metric, source, period, and scenario. Replace unresolved bindings with the correct target-client metrics before sharing or using the Page in an agent run.

Use a deliberately different test value in a second QA client when checking a reusable template. Both clients should display their own values.

## Version agent workflows deliberately

Agent templates use published revisions and client deployments. Editing a draft is different from publishing a revision or choosing that revision for a client. Review the deployment's Skill revisions, schedule, sources, and destinations before activation.

Saving a Page template does not schedule an agent or authorize delivery. See [Agents](/concepts/agents) and [Sharing and access](/reference/sharing-and-access).
