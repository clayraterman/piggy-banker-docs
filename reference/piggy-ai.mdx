# Lil Piggy: your client-scoped finance companion

Lil Piggy is a private notepad and grounded finance assistant that stays available while you work inside a client workspace. It is supplemental to the five client destinations, not a sixth destination or a second finance database.

## What a session contains

A Lil Piggy session can contain:

- a durable line-based notepad,
- a chat thread,
- calculations and metric references, and
- generated, reviewable artifacts.

Sessions belong to the signed-in member and current client. They remain private until selected material is explicitly saved to shared client notes.

## What Lil Piggy is good at

Use it to:

- answer questions grounded in the client's Context and finance evidence,
- calculate or compare saved measures,
- investigate a variance,
- draft a narrative or reporting artifact,
- navigate to a relevant product surface,
- prepare a supported action for review, or
- route a repeatable task into the Agents system.

Example requests:

- “Explain the change in gross margin and show the source periods.”
- “Calculate runway under the current base-case assumptions.”
- “Draft the finance section of this week's CFO brief.”
- “Take me to the source data behind Ending cash.”
- “Prepare this as an agent workflow; do not send anything yet.”

## Grounding and recalculation

Metric chips and calculations are reevaluated from canonical statements, saved metrics, and reusable inputs. An old pasted number is not treated as current merely because it appears in chat.

Lil Piggy should identify assumptions, source periods, and missing evidence. It does not turn unavailable data into zero.

## Actions and permissions

Lil Piggy uses a bounded server-owned command and tool catalog. It cannot invent permissions, select arbitrary destinations, or bypass approvals.

- Read-oriented users can ask questions and calculate within their access.
- Writes require the relevant client permission.
- Slack or email actions require explicit confirmation and the normal approval/dispatch path.
- Finance connections remain read-oriented; Lil Piggy does not write journal entries or mutate a connected ledger.
- Voice and transcription are not currently available.

## Save deliberately

Use **Save to client notes** only for material that should become shared Context. Working notes, tentative reasoning, and personal reminders can remain private in the session.

<Warning>
  Lil Piggy can accelerate judgment, but it should not hide judgment. Review formulas, sources, assumptions, and the exact payload of any proposed external action.
</Warning>
