## How to Talk to Piggy

Access Piggy through the **chat sidebar** — it's always visible on the right side of every portal page. Type your request in natural language, the same way you'd ask a question to a colleague. You do not need to learn any special syntax or know which menu to navigate.

Start with the outcome you want, not the technical action you think is required:

- Instead of _"Create a table block with variables"_ → try _"Build a P&L dashboard showing revenue, gross margin, and operating expenses for the last 12 months"_
- Instead of _"Set a trigger"_ → try _"Send me a Slack message every Monday with our cash balance and burn rate"_

Piggy uses the current page you have open, your connected data sources, and any [Business Context](/concepts/context) you've provided to interpret your requests. The more specific you are, the better the result.

## What Piggy Reads vs. Changes

Not every request Piggy receives changes anything — and understanding the difference helps you stay in control.

**Read-only requests** let Piggy inspect existing data and return an explanation or analysis without modifying anything in your portal:

- _"Why did gross margin drop in March?"_
- _"Has our QuickBooks sync finished?"_
- _"What is our current cash runway?"_

**Change requests** ask Piggy to create, update, or delete something in your portal — a metric, a dashboard page, an agent, or a setting:

- _"Create a new metric for Monthly Recurring Revenue"_
- _"Add a bar chart comparing this month's revenue to last month's"_
- _"Schedule a weekly variance report to Slack"_

When Piggy is about to make a change, it will describe what it intends to do before it acts. If the proposed change isn't what you intended, tell Piggy to stop or adjust its approach before it proceeds.

## Best Practices

The most effective prompts name a specific metric or page, include a time range, and describe the output format you want.

| Instead of this… | Try this |
|---|---|
| _"Show me the data"_ | _"Show me Monthly Revenue and Gross Margin for the last 6 months on a line chart"_ |
| _"Check our cash"_ | _"What is our current Cash Runway based on last month's burn rate? Alert me if it falls below 6 months."_ |
| _"Run the usual report"_ | _"Pull the board report: Revenue, Gross Margin, and Cash for Q1, compared to Q1 of last year"_ |
| _"Fix the numbers"_ | _"Our Burn Rate metric is showing a positive number — it should be negative. Can you check the formula?"_ |

When Piggy's first response isn't quite right, follow up with corrections rather than starting a new conversation. Piggy remembers the context of your current chat session and will refine its output based on your feedback.

## Context Matters

Piggy's answers are only as good as the context it has access to. It draws on two sources of background information:

1. **Business Context** — durable facts about the client's business that you've set in the portal, such as fiscal year, industry, and key operational assumptions. See [Business Context](/concepts/context) to learn how to set this up.
2. **The current page** — whatever dashboard or page you have open when you ask a question. Piggy uses what's visible on-screen to inform its interpretation of your request.

If Piggy returns an answer that seems off, check that the right client portal is open and that Business Context is filled in for that client.

<Note>
  Piggy is an AI and can make mistakes — especially with complex multi-step calculations or unfamiliar data structures. Always review important financial figures before sharing them with clients or stakeholders.
</Note>
