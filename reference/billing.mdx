# Billing and usage

Piggy Banker's pricing model combines a predictable subscription with optional additional usage.

## CFO teams

The pricing direction is:

- a flat fee for each billable managed client, and
- optional additional usage when the included allowance is not enough.

Billing belongs to the firm account. Firm owners manage the subscription and paid expansion.

In the current managed-client lifecycle, clients in billing-pending, onboarding, and active states count toward subscription quantity.

## Solo members (planned)

The intended solo plan is:

- one flat subscription, and
- optional additional usage.

<Note>
  **Availability:** A separate solo account and billing model does not exist today. Exact future prices, included allowances, and usage rates should be taken from the current checkout or agreement—not inferred from these docs.
</Note>

## Usage principles

- Usage is measured within the billed firm or solo account.
- Included AI allowance is pooled at the firm level in the current managed-client model.
- Optional overage is owner-controlled.
- Paid expansion fails closed when billing readiness cannot be verified.
- Past-due status can preserve read access while blocking paid expansion or other paid actions.

## What these docs do not promise

There is no universal promise here about free seats, credits per sync, automatic reload, renewal timing, or a specific spending cap. Those are commercial terms and product controls that must be shown explicitly before they are relied on.

For billing changes, use the billing controls available to the firm owner or contact the Piggy Banker team.
