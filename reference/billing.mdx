Review billing and usage before adding paid work or enabling an agent schedule. The current checkout and your agreement are the authority for prices, included allowances, and usage rates.

## Account ownership

Both **Company** and **CFO Firm** accounts exist. A Company owns its company workspace. A CFO Firm owns its managed-client portfolio. Billing and usage controls belong to the owning customer account and are limited by role.

Account creation and a finished finance setup are separate milestones. An account owner may need to complete billing before adding a managed client or enabling paid expansion. If a workflow asks for billing setup, use the account's Settings controls and review the checkout before confirming.

## Agent usage

A manual agent test, model-assisted editing, or scheduled run can consume model usage. A test that does not dispatch externally is not necessarily free. Review the selected model, account allowance, and any overage controls before running it.

An external delivery approval is different from permission to incur model usage. Keep schedules disabled while testing a new workflow.

## Questions to settle before activation

- Who owns the subscription and can change it?
- What is included in the current plan or agreement?
- What happens when the included allowance is used?
- Is additional usage enabled, and who can enable it?
- Which client or account will receive the usage?
- Does the account show a billing issue that blocks the requested action?

Do not infer a price, spending cap, free allowance, or renewal term from an example in these docs. If the checkout or account controls do not answer a billing question, contact the Piggy Banker team before enabling recurring work.
