Piggy Banker uses a flat-fee model — you pay a base fee per client (for CFO teams) or a flat monthly fee (for solo users), plus any extra usage you need. There are no surprise per-seat charges or hidden per-query fees outside of your plan's usage allotment, and you can top up or auto-reload credits at any time.

## Plan Types

**CFO Team Plan**

Designed for accounting firms and fractional CFO teams managing multiple clients. You pay a flat fee for each active client portal in your workspace. As your client list grows, each new portal adds one unit to your bill. You can also purchase extra usage add-ons when your team's AI query volume exceeds your plan's allotment.

**Solo Plan**

Designed for individual CFOs, controllers, or finance professionals managing a single company. You pay one flat monthly fee that covers your portal, regardless of how many team members you invite. Extra usage add-ons are available if you exceed your plan's included credits.

## What Counts as Usage

Your plan includes a monthly allotment of usage credits. The following actions consume credits:

- **AI queries to Piggy** — every message you send in the chat sidebar draws from your usage balance
- **Agent runs** — each time a Skill activates or a Trigger fires, it consumes credits proportional to the complexity of the task
- **Automated reports** — scheduled Trigger deliveries count against usage when they run
- **Data syncs** — periodic syncs from connected integrations (such as QuickBooks or Xero) consume a small amount of usage per sync

Piggy reports your usage as a percentage of your plan's monthly allotment. You can also see your remaining extra-usage balance if you've purchased additional credits.

## Checking Your Usage

Navigate to **Settings → Billing** to see:

- Your current plan, price, and renewal date
- AI usage consumed this month as a percentage of your allotment
- Remaining extra-usage credits
- Whether auto-reload is enabled and your current monthly spending limit

You can also ask Piggy directly:

> "What is my current usage this month?"

> "How many AI credits do I have left?"

> "When does my plan renew?"

Piggy will read your billing status and report it in plain language. It cannot make any changes to your plan or credits — those actions happen in **Settings → Billing**.

## Adding Extra Usage

If your workspace runs out of usage credits, Piggy will explain the situation and pause further AI requests until credits are restored. To add more:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Select **Purchase Additional Usage** and choose a credit amount
3. Credits are added immediately and available for use

To avoid running out, enable **auto-reload** in **Settings → Billing**. Auto-reload automatically purchases a set amount of extra credits when your balance falls below a threshold you define. You can also set a monthly spending cap to control how much auto-reload can spend in a given month.

<Note>
  If a monthly spending cap is preventing a credit purchase, increase or remove the cap in **Settings → Billing → Usage** before trying again.
</Note>

## Managing Clients on a CFO Plan

Each client portal counts as one billed client on a CFO Team Plan. Your bill is calculated based on the number of active client portals at your next renewal date.

- **Adding a client** — creating a new client portal increases your client count immediately. The additional charge is applied at your next renewal.
- **Removing a client** — archiving or deleting a client portal decreases your client count. The reduction is reflected at your next renewal.

If you need to temporarily pause work on a client without losing their data or incurring a full billing charge, contact support to discuss archive options.

<Note>
  Piggy can explain your current plan, usage balance, and billing status in plain language — but it cannot make billing changes on your behalf. All plan changes, credit purchases, and auto-reload settings must be made directly in **Settings → Billing**.
</Note>
