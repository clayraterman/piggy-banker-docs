Workspace settings control your team, connections, billing, and personalization options — access them from **Settings** in the top navigation. Most settings apply across your entire workspace rather than to a single client portal, so changes here affect everyone on your team.

## General Settings

Configure the foundational properties of your workspace:

- **Workspace name** — the display name shown across your portal and in any share links. Renaming your workspace also regenerates its URL slug, so existing links built from the old slug will change.
- **Fiscal year start month** — tells Piggy and your metrics which month begins your clients' fiscal year. Set this correctly before building any year-to-date or annual comparisons.
- **Default currency** — the currency used as the default when displaying financial metrics across your portals.

## Team Settings

Manage who has access to your workspace:

- **View team members** — see everyone currently in your workspace, their role, and any pending invitations.
- **Invite new members** — send an email invitation to add a new team member. Choose their role before sending.
- **Roles** — Piggy Banker has two workspace roles:
  - **Admin** — full access to all portals, settings, billing, and team management
  - **Viewer** — read-only access to shared pages and dashboards; cannot edit metrics, create agents, or change settings

<Note>
  If you want to share a report with someone outside your team without granting them full workspace access, use the **Share** control on the relevant page instead of inviting them as a team member.
</Note>

## Connections

Manage the data integrations that feed your client portals:

- **View active connections** — see all currently connected integrations and their sync status
- **Add an integration** — connect a new General Ledger, HR platform, spreadsheet, or database
- **Remove an integration** — disconnect a source you no longer need; historical data already synced will remain in the portal
- **Check sync status** — see when each integration last synced and whether any errors occurred

You can also ask Piggy about connection status: _"Has our QuickBooks sync finished?"_ or _"Are any of our integrations showing errors?"_

## Notifications

Control how and when Piggy Banker alerts you:

- **Email notifications** — choose which events trigger an email: agent run completions, sync failures, billing alerts, and team activity
- **Slack notifications** — connect your Slack workspace and choose a default channel or DM for system alerts
- **Alert thresholds** — set the metric thresholds that trigger proactive alerts from Piggy, such as cash runway falling below a set number of months

## Billing

Review your plan, usage balance, and payment details, or purchase additional usage credits. See [Billing and Plans](/reference/billing) for a full breakdown of how Piggy Banker billing works.

<Tip>
  You can ask Piggy to update most workspace settings directly in the chat sidebar — for example: _"Rename this workspace to Acme Corp Financials"_ or _"Set the fiscal year start month to April."_ Piggy will confirm the change before applying it, so you stay in control of what gets updated.
</Tip>
