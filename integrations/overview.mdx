# Connections and source-data reference

Connections authorize providers. Source-data readiness determines whether their finance domains can be used.

## Provider roles

| Provider type | Intended role | Does it replace the ledger? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QuickBooks Online | Accounting and general-ledger source | Yes, when its required ledger domain is ready |
| Puzzle | Accounting and general-ledger source | Yes, when its required ledger domain is ready |
| Xero | Accounting and general-ledger source | Yes, when its required ledger domain is ready |
| Bank/card connection | Balance and bank-transaction observation where supported | No |

Provider availability is rollout-dependent. **Connections** in the current workspace is the authority for providers that can be authorized now.

## Connection state versus domain state

A provider may be authorized while one of its datasets is partial, stale, failed, unsupported, or missing. Downstream metrics should use only a selected source whose required domain is ready.

## Source selection

Piggy Banker selects exactly one ready owner per finance domain. This prevents two connected ledgers or a ledger plus fallback bridge from doubling the same financial activity.

Alternates remain useful for:

- reconciliation,
- coverage comparison,
- migration,
- fallback, and
- entity-specific models.

They are not silently aggregated.

## Typical accounting domains

Where a provider supports them, normalized finance data may include:

- accounts,
- balances and statements,
- accounting transactions,
- ledger transactions and lines,
- customers and vendors,
- invoices and bills,
- payments and applications.

Bank sources have a different contract: observed balances and supported bank transactions, with their own as-of times.

## Lineage

Every downstream value should retain enough source metadata to answer:

- Which client and entity?
- Which provider and linked account?
- Which finance domain?
- Which source period or as-of time?
- Which sync produced it?
- Was the source ready and fresh?
- Which metric definition or formula transformed it?

## Connection checklist

1. Authorize the correct provider account.
2. Select the intended company, tenant, or accounts.
3. Wait for sync and normalization.
4. Review domain readiness and freshness.
5. Confirm selected source ownership.
6. Reconcile a sample period.
7. Only then build dependent metrics or schedules.
