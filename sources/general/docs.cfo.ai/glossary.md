# Source: https://docs.cfo.ai/glossary

These concepts describe how cfo.ai turns source data and business assumptions into a living financial Model.

## 

[​](https://docs.cfo.ai/glossary#your-workspace)

Your workspace

## Workspace

Your company’s home in cfo.ai. A workspace contains its financial Model, Pages, Scenarios, connected sources, team, and settings.

## Ari

Your AI finance coworker. Ari can answer business questions, explain the supporting data, and build or update a Model you can inspect and change.

## Model

A shared, living representation of how your business changes over time. It combines connected data, Variables, Dimensions, formulas, assumptions, and Scenarios that you and Ari can inspect.

## Integration

A connection that brings data from another system, such as accounting software, an HR platform, a spreadsheet, or a database, into cfo.ai.

## Database

A structured collection of source records organized into rows and columns.

## 

[​](https://docs.cfo.ai/glossary#pages-and-reports)

Pages and reports

## Page

A canvas that organizes related Table Blocks, charts, and written context into a report or working view.

## Table Block

A live, spreadsheet-like grid that shows Variables across time and lets you inspect, edit, segment, and compare their values.

## 

[​](https://docs.cfo.ai/glossary#modeling-concepts)

Modeling concepts

## Variable

A named line of your Model, such as Revenue, Headcount, Cash, or Gross Margin. Its values can be imported, entered, or calculated.

## Dimension

A category used to group, filter, or segment a Variable, such as Department, Region, Customer, Product, or Date.

## Dimension item

One entry within a Dimension. For example, Sales is an item in Department, and West is an item in Region.

## Segment

A slice of a Variable’s data identified by one or more Dimension items, such as Revenue for Sales or Revenue for Sales in the West.

## Cell address

The complete identity of one Model value: its Variable, one item from each applicable Dimension, and its time period.

## Formula

A calculation that determines a Variable’s value. Its expression says what to calculate, and its scope says where the calculation applies.

## Formula range

A span of the timeline that can use its own formula. Actuals and Forecast are common formula ranges.

## Last close

The date that separates closed Actuals from the Forecast. Updating Last close moves that boundary without rewriting your formulas.

## Scenario

A version of your Model used to explore an alternative plan or compare possible outcomes. Main is the primary working Scenario.

## Snapshot

A read-only copy of a Scenario at a specific point in time. Use a Snapshot to preserve a submitted plan or a board-reporting baseline.

## 

[​](https://docs.cfo.ai/glossary#automation)

Automation

## Skill

A reusable workflow that teaches Ari how your team approaches a recurring kind of work.

## Trigger

A scheduled instruction that asks Ari to run a recurring task at the time you choose.

## Guide

An interactive walkthrough that highlights Pages, blocks, or cells and explains what someone should notice.
