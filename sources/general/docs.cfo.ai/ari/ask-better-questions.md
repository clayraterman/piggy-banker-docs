# Source: https://docs.cfo.ai/ari/ask-better-questions

Ari works best when it understands the decision behind your request. Start with the outcome you want, then add context only when it changes the answer.

## 

[​](https://docs.cfo.ai/ari/ask-better-questions#name-the-business-question)

Name the business question

Instead of asking for a generic report, describe what you want to learn.

> Which departments drove the increase in operating expenses last month?

> How many sales hires can we add while keeping at least 12 months of cash runway?

> Which customer segments explain the gap between our Forecast and Actuals?

## 

[​](https://docs.cfo.ai/ari/ask-better-questions#add-the-time-period-and-baseline)

Add the time period and baseline

Say which period matters and what Ari should compare it with.

> Compare March Actuals with the March Forecast and with February Actuals.

> Show the next four quarters against the Main Scenario.

If you are working in a different Scenario, name it when that choice matters to the result.

## 

[​](https://docs.cfo.ai/ari/ask-better-questions#ask-for-the-level-of-detail-you-need)

Ask for the level of detail you need

Use the business categories that explain the result, such as department, customer, product, employee, entity, or region.

> Break payroll down by department, then show the employees behind the largest change.

> Show revenue by customer segment and plan, including new customers, expansion, and churn.

When a number is already visible, point Ari to the current Page, table, row, or cell.

## 

[​](https://docs.cfo.ai/ari/ask-better-questions#say-whether-you-want-an-answer-or-a-change)

Say whether you want an answer or a change

For analysis:

> Explain the change in gross margin without modifying the Model.

For a saved change:

> Update the Forecast formula for gross margin in the Downside Scenario and show me the resulting values.

For a review before saving:

> Draft the formula and check whether it is valid, but wait before applying it.

## 

[​](https://docs.cfo.ai/ari/ask-better-questions#ask-ari-to-show-its-work)

Ask Ari to show its work

Useful follow-ups include:

- “Which source data supports that conclusion?”
- “Which assumptions did you make?”
- “What changed compared with the prior period?”
- “Which formula applies to this cell?”
- “Can you reconcile this total to the detail below it?”
- “Where can I change the assumption and compare another outcome?”

When Ari creates or updates a Page, open it and inspect the same Model behind the answer. You can adjust the assumptions yourself or ask Ari to save a different case in a Scenario.
