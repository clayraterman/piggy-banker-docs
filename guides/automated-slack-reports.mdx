# Send a scheduled financial report to Slack

This is Piggy Banker's flagship end-to-end workflow: generate a repeatable CFO report from current client evidence, review the exact outbound message, approve it, and retain delivery history.

<Note>
  **Current behavior:** Production Slack delivery is human-approved. The workflow consumes canonical finance data, saved metrics, and Context; it does not yet send a rendered Page snapshot. Policy-controlled unattended delivery is future work.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the finance source ready">
    Connect and sync the required general ledger. Add bank/card feeds only when current balance observations improve the report. Resolve stale, partial, or missing required domains.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the reporting primitives">
    Create and reconcile the saved metrics the report needs. Record source periods, formula references, assumptions, and known omissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Slack">
    Authorize the client's Slack connection and choose a client default channel or an agent-specific destination override.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate the destination">
    Send a real validation message. When Slack posting is approval-gated, validation first creates a proposal that an eligible reviewer must approve before dispatch. A mock delivery is not proof that production credentials and channel access work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy the reporting workflow">
    Start with the Weekly CFO Brief template or another approved cadence template. Select the immutable revision for this client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the deployment">
    Resolve Context, reporting style, Skills, destination, UTC schedule, and approval policy. Review inherited defaults and explicit overrides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a manual test">
    The test reads current finance evidence and saves a reviewable in-app artifact without creating approvals or sending externally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the artifact">
    Verify periods, units, metric definitions, formulas, assumptions, omissions, narrative, and proposed call to action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the schedule">
    Eligible UTC schedule slots enter the queue only after readiness checks pass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the proposed Slack action">
    The scheduled run produces an evidence receipt, report artifact, and exact channel-bound message payload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve with separation of duties">
    A different eligible human approves or rejects the proposal. Approval queues dispatch; it is not delivery confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track delivery">
    Agent Activity retains status, Slack channel and message identifiers, failures, and retries. Provider retries reuse a stable message identity to avoid duplicate posts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended report contract

A strong recurring brief includes:

- explicit source and comparison periods,
- cash and liquidity,
- P&L or operating performance,
- saved KPI series,
- runway or forecast evidence,
- material variances,
- assumptions and omissions,
- owner-ready actions, and
- a clear operator-review requirement.

## Before relying on the schedule

- Reconcile one manual run to the books.
- Confirm that the destination belongs to the correct client.
- Test the exact report format with its audience.
- Define who reviews approvals when the usual owner is unavailable.
- Keep stale or missing required data visible instead of sending a confident but ungrounded narrative.
