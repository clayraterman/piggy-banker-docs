Headcount is almost always the largest single expense on the P&L — and the hardest to track accurately when data is spread across HR systems, spreadsheets, and offer letters. Piggy Banker brings everything together: connect your HR system (or enter data manually), and you'll have a live headcount dashboard that shows current staff, payroll cost by department, and the real-time impact of any planned hire. This guide walks you through setup, dashboard construction, and forward-looking hire modeling.

## Connecting HR Data

**Option 1 — Connect an HR system**

If you use an HR platform like Rippling, navigate to **Settings** → **Connections** and select your HR system from the integrations directory. Complete the authorization flow. Once connected, Piggy Banker pulls in employee records — name, department, pay rate, start date, termination date, employment type, and more — and keeps them in sync automatically.

Piggy Banker requires at minimum: **employee name**, **department**, **pay rate**, **start date**, and **termination date**. These fields are needed to calculate who is currently active and what payroll cost to attribute to each period.

**Option 2 — Enter headcount data manually**

If you don't have a connected HR system, create a manual data source under **Connections** → **Manual Entry**. Enter each employee as a row with their role, department, start date, and monthly cost. You can update this list whenever someone joins or leaves.

## Core Headcount Metrics to Create

Build these four metrics to cover the essential headcount picture:

**Total Headcount**
A count of active employees across the entire organization, filtered to records where the termination date is blank or in the future. Updates automatically as your HR system syncs.

**Headcount by Department**
The same active-employee count segmented by department dimension. Use this metric in charts and tables to see how headcount is distributed — and how it shifts over time as you hire and restructure.

**Monthly Payroll Cost**
The total payroll expense for the current month, sourced from your GL's payroll accounts or calculated from the HR roster (pay rate × active employees). If employees have different pay periods (weekly, biweekly, monthly), provide a pay period mapping so each employee's cost is derived correctly.

**Loaded Cost per Employee**
A formula metric: `Monthly Payroll Cost ÷ Total Headcount`. This gives you a blended average that helps you spot when cost-per-head is rising faster than headcount — a common early warning for comp inflation or benefits cost escalation.

## Building a Headcount Dashboard

1. Go to **Pages** → **New Page** and name it `Headcount` (or `Team & Payroll`).
2. Add **Metric Widgets** for Total Headcount, Monthly Payroll Cost, and Loaded Cost per Employee at the top of the page — these are the three numbers leadership checks most often.
3. Add a **Table** block with the following columns for the employee detail view:

   | Name | Role | Department | Start Date | Monthly Cost |
   |---|---|---|---|---|

   One row per active employee. Sort by department, then by name within each department.

4. Add a second **Table** block below for the department summary view:

   | Department | Headcount | Monthly Payroll | % of Total Payroll |
   |---|---|---|---|

5. Add a **Stacked Bar Chart** with Headcount by Department over time. Use the trailing 12 months as the window. This makes it easy to see which teams are growing fastest and how the mix has shifted.

## Modeling a New Hire

Before you commit to a new hire, use Piggy Banker to see the exact payroll and cash impact. Create a **planned hire metric** — a manual metric with future monthly values set to the new employee's fully loaded monthly cost starting on their projected start date. Then add that metric to your Cash Flow or Payroll dashboards to see how it changes your burn rate, runway, and ending cash for each month going forward.

For a more powerful scenario analysis, try asking Piggy directly:

```text
If we hire two engineers in Q3 at $150k each,
what happens to our monthly burn rate and cash runway?
Show me the effect on payroll and ending cash for each month.
```

Piggy will model the impact, show you the month-by-month delta, and surface the exact month where your runway crosses any threshold you care about — all without touching your live plan.

<Note>
  For the most accurate headcount modeling, add a **Context** entry with your team structure, hiring plan, and compensation philosophy. Context is durable business information that informs Piggy's answers across every conversation — so you don't have to re-explain your org chart every time you ask a headcount question.
</Note>
