# Model headcount and payroll impact

A finance-owned headcount model connects staffing decisions to payroll, operating expense, cash, and forecast outcomes.

<Note>
  **Pages availability:** The finance-modeling contract in this guide applies now. Composing it in the final free-form Pages editor is coming soon.
</Note>

<Note>
  Piggy Banker does not currently promise a general HR-system connector. Use trusted client inputs and available finance sources; label manual and forecast data clearly.
</Note>

## Define the model grain

Choose one of these grains deliberately:

- employee,
- open role,
- department,
- role family, or
- monthly aggregate.

Employee-level detail gives precision but increases privacy and maintenance requirements. Use the least granular model that supports the decision.

## Suggested inputs

Depending on the model, inputs may include:

- department or cost center,
- role and employment type,
- start and end dates,
- base compensation,
- payroll tax and benefit load,
- bonus or commission assumption,
- recruiting or equipment cost,
- currency, and
- status such as active, planned, or backfill.

Store shared scalar rates as reusable inputs. Store scenario-specific hiring timing and amounts as forecast assumptions.

## Calculated measures

Useful metrics include:

- active headcount,
- monthly payroll,
- fully loaded people cost,
- planned hires,
- annualized payroll,
- payroll as a percentage of revenue,
- department people cost, and
- forecast cash impact.

## Connect to actual finance data

Use payroll expense from the ready general ledger as the actual financial anchor. Reconcile the modeled historical cost to the books before relying on future periods.

Do not claim that a manually maintained roster is live provider data. Preserve owner, effective date, and review cadence for every manual input.

## Suggested Page

A headcount Page can combine:

1. current headcount and payroll,
2. hires and departures by month,
3. department cost,
4. forecast versus budget,
5. cash or runway impact,
6. scenario comparison, and
7. decisions requiring approval.

## Controls

Protect personal data, restrict client access appropriately, and avoid placing sensitive employee detail in a broadly shared Page or agent message.
