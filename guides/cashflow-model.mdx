# Design a cash flow model

“Cash flow” can mean three different things. Keep them separate before composing the model.

| Layer | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Accounting cash flow** | Period activity reported from the books |
| **Current balance observations** | Point-in-time bank or card balances with provider timestamps |
| **Cash forecast** | Future receipts, payments, and ending cash driven by assumptions |

## Choose the decision

Decide whether the model supports weekly liquidity management, a 13-week forecast, monthly board reporting, or runway planning. The answer determines the grain, source periods, and assumptions.

## Build the metric spine

A practical model may include:

- opening cash,
- operating cash inflows,
- payroll and operating outflows,
- investing and financing flows,
- net cash change,
- ending cash,
- minimum cash policy,
- forecast ending cash, and
- runway or funding gap.

Bind accounting actuals to ready ledger references. Use observed bank balances as separately labeled points of comparison, not as replacements for statement history.

## Forecast deliberately

Forecast assumptions might cover collections timing, payroll, vendor payments, financing events, or minimum cash. Give each assumption an owner, effective period, scenario, and rationale.

For a rolling model, preserve the relationship:

```text
opening cash + inflows - outflows = ending cash
next period opening cash = prior period ending cash
```

Treat that as a model invariant and test it across every period.

## Compose the Page

A decision-ready cash Page can include:

1. current liquidity and its as-of time,
2. forecast ending cash by week,
3. minimum-cash threshold,
4. major inflow and outflow drivers,
5. variance from the prior forecast,
6. scenario assumptions, and
7. actions with owners.

## Validation

- Reconcile historical ending cash to the accounting statement.
- Compare observed bank balances without forcing them to equal book cash.
- Keep credit-card balances out of cash on hand.
- Test missing periods and zero inflows.
- Verify multi-entity and currency treatment.
- Show whether runway is actual, forecast, or scenario-derived.

<Note>
  This is a modeling pattern, not a promise that every source, Page block, or alert control is currently exposed in the UI.
</Note>
