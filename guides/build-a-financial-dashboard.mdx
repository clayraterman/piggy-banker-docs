Turn your live metric data into a shareable, always-current financial dashboard in minutes. Pages in Piggy Banker are Notion-style canvases made up of blocks — metric widgets, charts, tables, and text — that update automatically as your underlying data changes. This guide walks you through building your first page from scratch.

## Prerequisites

- At least one metric created and connected to a data source. See [Create Metrics](/setup/create-metrics) if you haven't done this yet.

## Build Your Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pages and create a new page">
    In your client portal, navigate to **Pages** in the left sidebar. Click **New Page** to open a blank canvas.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your page">
    Give the page a clear, descriptive name. Good examples:

    - `Executive Summary`
    - `Monthly P&L`
    - `Cash Dashboard`
    - `Q3 Board Report`

    You can rename the page at any time by clicking its title.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a metric widget">
    Click the **+** button that appears when you hover between or below blocks, then select **Metric Widget**. Choose the metric you want to display from the list. The widget renders immediately with the live current value and updates automatically on every data sync.

    Add as many metric widgets as you need — they stack and reflow to fill the available width.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a chart">
    Click **+** → **Chart**, then choose the metric (or metrics) you want to visualize. Select a chart type:

    | Chart type | Best for |
    |---|---|
    | **Line** | Trends over time (revenue, cash balance) |
    | **Bar** | Period-over-period comparisons |
    | **Stacked bar** | Part-to-whole breakdowns (expenses by department) |
    | **Scorecard** | A single headline number with a delta vs. target |

    Charts pull live data from your model — they are never static snapshots.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a text block">
    Click **+** → **Text** to insert a rich-text block. Use text blocks to add narrative commentary, label a section, or provide context for the numbers directly above or below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rearrange your blocks">
    Drag any block by its handle (the six-dot icon that appears on hover at the left edge) to reorder it on the page. Rearranging a block preserves all of its settings — you never have to rebuild it after moving it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and share (optional)">
    Pages save automatically as you build. To share a finished page with a client or team member, click the **Share** button in the top-right corner and choose your visibility settings. See [Pages and Dashboards](/concepts/pages-and-dashboards) for details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Block Types

Every block you add to a page serves a distinct purpose. Here is a quick reference:

**Metric Widget**
Displays a single named metric as a live KPI tile — current value, label, and optional trend indicator. Use it to surface the numbers your audience needs to read first.

**Chart**
Renders one or more metrics as a visual: line, bar, stacked bar, or scorecard. Use a chart when the shape of the story (a trend, a comparison, a breakdown) communicates more than rows of numbers.

**Table**
A live grid built from your metrics and dimensions. Use a table when readers need the underlying numbers in rows and columns — for example, a full P&L, a variance report, or a departmental expense breakdown. You can format rows, add comparison columns, sort, and hide individual rows without changing the underlying data.

**Text**
Unstructured prose for narrative, context, or section headers. Text blocks do not contain data — they sit alongside your other blocks to explain what the numbers mean.

## Using Templates

If your workspace has a Playbook set up for a common dashboard type (Monthly P&L, Board Deck, Cash Report), you can start from that template instead of building from scratch. Go to **Pages** → **New from Playbook**, select the template, and the page will be pre-populated with the right blocks and metric wiring. Customize from there.

<Tip>
  Ask Piggy to build a dashboard for you. Try: *"Build me a monthly P&L dashboard with revenue, gross margin, operating expenses, and net income."* Piggy will create the page, add the right blocks, and wire up your metrics automatically.
</Tip>
