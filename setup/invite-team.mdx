Great financial work is a team effort. Piggy Banker lets you bring your entire CFO team into a shared workspace — giving each person the right level of access to the clients they support — and share read-only views with clients so they can follow along without needing a full account.

## Team Roles

Every person you invite gets one of two roles. Choose based on what they need to do in the workspace:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield-check">
    Full access to all client portals, data connections, metrics, agents, pages, and workspace settings. Use this role for CFO team members who actively manage clients, build dashboards, or configure integrations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Viewer" icon="eye">
    Read-only access to the pages and dashboards assigned to them. Viewers cannot edit metrics, modify connections, or change any settings. Use this role for team members or junior staff who need to review data but not manage it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Admins can see and manage all client workspaces. Viewers only see the specific clients they've been granted access to.

## Invite a Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team settings">
    Click **Settings** in the left sidebar of your workspace, then select **Team** from the settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Invite Member'">
    Click the **Invite Member** button at the top of the Team page. An invitation dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email and select a role">
    Type the team member's work email address, then choose their role — **Admin** or **Viewer**. For Viewer accounts, you can also select which client portals they should have access to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. Piggy Banker sends them an email with a secure link to accept the invitation and set up their account. The invitation is valid for 7 days — you can resend it from the Team page if it expires.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once they accept, the team member appears in your Team list with their assigned role. You can update their role or access at any time.

## Client Access

Your clients don't need a Piggy Banker account to view their financial dashboards. You have two ways to share their data with them:

- **Shared Page Links** — Generate a read-only shareable link directly from any Page (dashboard). Anyone with the link can view the page without signing in. Use this for regular reporting cadences, board packages, or quick check-ins.
- **Client Portal Invitations** — Invite a client directly to their portal as a Viewer. This gives them a persistent login they can return to anytime, rather than a static link.

Both options are strictly read-only. Clients cannot edit metrics, access other clients' data, or change any workspace settings.

## Removing Access

When a team member leaves your firm or no longer needs access, revoke their access immediately:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Find the team member in the list.
3. Click the **···** menu next to their name and select **Remove Member**.

Their access is revoked instantly. Any shared page links they generated remain active unless you manually expire them from the relevant Page's share settings.

<Note>
  **Seat limits apply to your plan.** Each active team member (Admin or Viewer) counts toward your workspace's seat allotment. Piggy Banker uses a flat fee per client plus additional usage charges — check **Settings → Billing** to review your current seat usage and plan limits, or ask Piggy to summarize your plan details for you.
</Note>
