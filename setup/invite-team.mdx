# Invite teammates and client collaborators

Access follows two scopes: the firm and an individual client. Grant the narrowest role that supports the person's work.

## Firm access

- **Firm owners** manage billing, firm users, and the full client portfolio.
- **Firm operators** work across assigned client operations without owning billing.

## Client access

Client roles are scoped to one business:

- **Client admins** manage that client's setup and collaborators.
- **Editors** contribute to permitted client work.
- **Viewers** have read-oriented access.

Exact controls shown in the product can vary as rollout continues. The permission enforced by the server—not a hidden button—is authoritative.

## Invite checklist

1. Confirm whether the person belongs at firm or client scope.
2. Choose the correct client before inviting a client collaborator.
3. Assign the least-privileged useful role.
4. Verify that destinations, Context, Pages, and agent artifacts remain client-scoped.
5. Remove or adjust access when responsibility changes.

## Portal boundary

The authenticated client portal is a separate shell. Do not treat it as the internal workspace with navigation hidden. This preserves a clear boundary between firm operations and the client experience.

<Warning>
  A person's ability to read finance data does not automatically grant permission to change connections, edit metrics, approve their own proposed action, or dispatch data externally.
</Warning>
