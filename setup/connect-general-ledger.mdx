# Connect the client's general ledger

A usable general ledger is required for Piggy Banker's core finance workflow. It supplies the accounting structure and historical activity needed for statements, metrics, models, and grounded analysis.

## Supported provider direction

Piggy Banker is designed to work with QuickBooks Online, Puzzle, and Xero accounting tenants. Provider availability can vary during rollout; the **Connections** page in your workspace is the authority for what can be connected now.

<Note>
  A successful authorization is only the first checkpoint. The ledger domain must finish syncing and report **ready** before downstream models should rely on it.
</Note>

## Connect safely

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the correct client">
    Confirm the client and legal entity before starting OAuth. A connection is client-scoped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the accounting provider">
    In **Connections**, select the provider that owns the books for this entity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the correct company or tenant">
    If the provider account contains several companies, select deliberately. Keep separate entities as separate linked accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the initial sync">
    Wait for normalization to complete. “Connected” without usable ledger records is not ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the source">
    Review the available domain and dataset status, freshness dates, record counts, and discovered dimensions. These diagnostics are API-backed during rollout and are not yet exposed as one complete source-data UI.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to verify

- the expected chart of accounts is present,
- statement periods match the client books,
- ledger detail covers the periods you will model,
- the correct entity owns the domain,
- no alternate source is being double counted, and
- unavailable provider data is shown as unavailable, not zero.

Some providers restrict specific datasets. For example, access to detailed journal data can depend on provider entitlements. Build only on domains that report ready.

<Note>
  The canonical source-data catalog can report readiness, counts, bounded date coverage, and discovered dimensions. A complete operator-facing diagnostics experience is coming with the Source data workflow.
</Note>

## Multiple ledgers

A client can have multiple accounting connections for separate entities. Piggy Banker retains each connection's lineage. Consolidation should be an explicit model with defined eliminations and currency treatment—not an accidental sum of every connected realm.
