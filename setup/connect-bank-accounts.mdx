# Connect optional bank and card accounts

Bank and card feeds are optional. Use them when current balance observations or bank-transaction visibility improve a client's liquidity view.

They complement the general ledger; they do not replace it.

## What these feeds are for

A bank-data connection can provide:

- observed account balances,
- account identity and type, and
- bank transaction observations where supported.

It does not provide the full accounting model needed for a P&L, balance sheet, cash-flow statement, AR, AP, or journal detail.

## Cash versus credit

- Deposit-account balances may contribute to a current liquidity watch view.
- Credit-card balances are liabilities or watch items.
- Never add a card balance to cash on hand.
- A provider balance and the ledger's book balance can have different timestamps and meanings; show both provenance and as-of date.

## Setup

1. Open **Connections** for the correct client.
2. Choose the available bank-data provider.
3. Authorize only the accounts needed for the finance model.
4. Confirm account type, currency, and ownership.
5. Review the observed as-of time and freshness.
6. Decide which balances should appear in a metric or Page.

<Warning>
  Do not describe a bank observation as “real-time” unless its provider timestamp supports that claim. Accounting statement values and bank balance observations often update on different cadences.
</Warning>

## Reconciliation

When a live observation differs from the books, preserve both values and investigate the timing or classification difference. Do not overwrite one source with the other simply to make a dashboard agree.
