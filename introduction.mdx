Piggy Banker connects your financial data, turns it into reusable Metrics, and brings it together in Pages and agent workflows.

Start with one question and one trusted source. Prove the numbers before you automate the narrative.

## Choose how you work

| Account | Where you start | What it manages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Company** | Your company workspace | Your business's sources, Context, Metrics, Pages, and agents |
| **CFO Firm** | Mission Control | A portfolio of separately scoped client workspaces and shared account resources |

Choose the account type at signup. A Company account does not need a managed-client portfolio. A CFO Firm enters each managed client's workspace to do that client's finance work.

## Your first complete workflow

```text
Connections → Source data → Metrics → Page → reviewed agent output
                              ↑
                    Context and assumptions
```

1. Authorize the right accounting company in **Connections** and check the sync.
2. Inspect **Source data** and reconcile a period with the provider.
3. Open **Metrics** inside **Pages** and save the measures you need.
4. Create a Page with those metrics and explanatory notes.
5. Test an agent with the same inputs and review its output before scheduling or delivery.

**Context** explains the business through Company, Money, and Strategy. It does not replace accounting records. **Lil Piggy** helps alongside the current workspace as a notepad and finance companion.

<Note>
Pages, the Metrics management window, account-owned Templates / Playbooks, and Company and CFO Firm signup are available. External financial MCP remains disabled by default and is not self-service. See [external agent access](/integrations/mcp-export) for the separate experimental browser-navigation boundary.
</Note>
