## How Piggy Banker Works

Every client has their own portal. Inside that portal, live data from your General Ledger feeds a metrics system. Those metrics power free-form dashboard pages. Those pages power automated agent workflows. The whole time, Piggy — your AI companion — is available to help you build, update, or ask questions about any of it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a client portal">
    CFO teams manage multiple clients from one dashboard. Add a new client and give Piggy the business context it needs to understand how the company works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your data sources">
    Connect your General Ledger (QuickBooks, Xero, or Puzzle) and optionally link bank accounts or credit cards for live balance visibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create metrics">
    Turn raw accounting data into structured metrics: P&L, cash flow, forecasts, headcount — whatever matters most to the business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build pages and automate reports">
    Arrange metrics into Notion-style dashboard pages and create agents to send automated Slack reports on any cadence you choose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Makes It Different

Most financial tools give you a report. Piggy Banker gives you a **living model**: data that auto-updates, metrics that agents can query, and dashboards that reflect reality right now — not last week's export.
