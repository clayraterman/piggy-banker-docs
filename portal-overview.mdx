# How Piggy Banker is organized

Piggy Banker has two operating levels: a **firm workspace** for managing a portfolio and an isolated **client workspace** for doing finance work for one business.

## Firm workspace

After login, a CFO team lands in its multi-tenant workspace. This level is designed to contain:

- a portfolio overview,
- firm-wide agent activity,
- the Templates / Playbooks library,
- billing and team administration, and
- the list of client workspaces.

Templates and Playbooks live here because they are reusable firm knowledge. They are not a sixth client destination.

## Client workspace

When you enter a client, the target navigation is exactly:

| Destination | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Tasks, alerts, and recent agent activity |
| **Context** | Durable Company, Money, and Strategy facts |
| **Pages** | Free-form finance models, dashboards, and metric management |
| **Agents** | Client deployments, schedules, skills, runs, and activity |
| **Connections** | Live provider connections and read-only source-data status |

<Note>
  **Pages rollout:** Pages is the decided home for composed finance views and metric management. During rollout, some workspaces may still expose older reporting, forecasting, planning, or dashboard surfaces. Those are compatibility internals, not additional destinations in the final app.
</Note>

## Lil Piggy is always nearby

Lil Piggy is a client-scoped side companion, not a destination. Its notepad, chat, calculations, and artifacts follow the current client and signed-in member. It can help investigate or prepare work without becoming a second source of truth.

## Client portal

The authenticated client portal is a separate, limited shell for client collaborators. It is not the internal workspace with navigation hidden by CSS. Access must remain scoped to the client and capabilities granted to that person.

## A simple mental model

- The **firm** owns the customer relationship, shared operating knowledge, and billing.
- The **client workspace** owns that business's Context, source data, metrics, Pages, and agent runs.
- The **portal** exposes approved client experiences through a separate shell.
- **Lil Piggy** helps inside the current scope but does not broaden it.
