Piggy Banker separates account administration from the finance work for one business.

## Company and CFO Firm accounts

A **Company** account opens its own company workspace directly. A **CFO Firm** starts in Mission Control and manages multiple client workspaces. Account type is chosen at signup; it is not inferred from the number of clients.

For a CFO Firm, Mission Control brings together client work and activity. Enter a client for its finance data, and use the return-to-Mission-Control navigation to return to the portfolio. Account settings contain the administration available to your role, including team, client, and billing controls.

## The five workspace destinations

| Destination | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Review tasks, alerts, recent activity, and first-use setup guidance |
| **Context** | Maintain Company, Money, and Strategy facts |
| **Pages** | Compose finance views and open Metrics management |
| **Agents** | Configure agents and Skills, review runs, and manage schedules |
| **Connections** | Authorize providers, inspect sync status, and open Source data |

Metrics is a window inside Pages. It includes **Metrics**, **Source data**, and **Dimensions**. Older reporting, forecasting, and planning URLs redirect into Pages rather than adding more destinations.

## Reusable account resources

**Templates & playbooks** opens the account's reusable Page library. A CFO Firm can browse it before creating its first client; applying content happens inside a client workspace. Agent templates are a separate workflow blueprint with published revisions and client deployments.

See [Templates and Playbooks](/concepts/playbooks) for the boundaries between reusable structure and private client data.

## Lil Piggy

Lil Piggy is a side companion within the current client workspace. Its notepad, chat, and artifacts follow that scope. It can help prepare work, but an instruction in chat does not grant access to another client or approve external delivery.

## The client portal is separate

The authenticated client portal gives permitted collaborators a separate, limited experience. It is distinct from both the internal workspace and a secret Page publication.

An operator's agent artifact does not automatically become a portal report. Approving a Slack message, publishing a Page link, and making a report available in the portal are separate actions. See [Sharing and access](/reference/sharing-and-access).
