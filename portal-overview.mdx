Every client you manage in Piggy Banker gets its own dedicated portal — a self-contained workspace that holds everything related to that client's finances. When you open a client portal, you'll find six sections that together cover the full lifecycle of financial management: staying informed, providing AI context, automating workflows, building reports, connecting data, and applying best-practice templates.

## Portal sections

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card
    title="Overview"
    icon="house"
    href="/concepts/client-portal"
  >
    Your at-a-glance home for the client. See outstanding tasks, active alerts, and a summary of recent agent activity — all in one place, so you always know what needs attention.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Context"
    icon="brain"
    href="/concepts/context"
  >
    Store durable facts about the business that Piggy uses every time it answers a question or runs an automation. Think of it as a living briefing document: industry, revenue model, KPIs, fiscal calendar, and anything else that shapes how the numbers should be interpreted.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Agents"
    icon="robot"
    href="/concepts/agents"
  >
    Build and manage automated workflows that run on a schedule or in response to triggers. Each agent combines skills — discrete AI-powered actions — to complete tasks like drafting a monthly commentary, flagging budget variances, or preparing a board-ready summary.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Pages"
    icon="layout-dashboard"
    href="/concepts/pages-and-dashboards"
  >
    Create Notion-style dashboards that mix live financial metrics, charts, tables, and written narrative. Pages pull directly from your connected General Ledger so every figure stays current without manual refreshes.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Connections"
    icon="plug"
    href="/integrations/overview"
  >
    Manage the data sources powering the portal. Connect and sync your General Ledger (QuickBooks, Xero, or Puzzle) along with any other data integrations. The General Ledger connection is required before you can build metrics or Pages.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Playbooks"
    icon="book-bookmark"
    href="/concepts/playbooks"
  >
    Apply pre-built templates and repeatable workflows to common financial tasks. Playbooks capture institutional knowledge — for example, a month-end close checklist or a variance analysis workflow — so your whole team operates consistently across every client.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The CFO Team Portal

If you're on a CFO Team plan, you also have access to a top-level portal that sits above all of your individual client portals. Use the CFO Team Portal to:

- **Monitor all clients at once** from a consolidated overview page that surfaces alerts and activity across your entire book of business.
- **Run team-level agents** that operate across multiple clients or handle practice-wide tasks, such as generating a weekly digest of all client variances.
- **Manage shared templates** so that playbooks and page layouts you perfect for one client can be pushed to others with a single action.
- **Access individual client portals** directly from the team dashboard — click any client to jump straight into their portal without losing your place.

The CFO Team Portal is your command center. Individual client portals are where the detailed work happens.

<Note>
  Every client portal is fully isolated. Data, pages, agents, and connections from one client are never visible to, or accessible from, another client's portal — even within the same CFO team account.
</Note>
