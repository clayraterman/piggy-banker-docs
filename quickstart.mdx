# Quickstart

Use this sequence for every client. The order matters: an agent is only as useful as the source data, definitions, and Context underneath it.

<Note>
  **Availability:** Client setup, connected finance data, metrics primitives, and controlled agent runs exist today. The final free-form Pages experience is coming soon; current workspaces may expose compatibility reporting surfaces during the transition.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the client workspace">
    Add the business under your firm and confirm who should have access. A client workspace is an isolated finance context, not a folder inside another client's data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add business Context">
    Complete **Company**, **Money**, and **Strategy**. Record durable facts such as the operating model, entities, fiscal conventions, cash policy, goals, and constraints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the general ledger">
    A general ledger is required for the core workflow. Choose the supported accounting provider shown in **Connections**, authorize the correct company or tenant, sync it, and verify that the ledger domain is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add optional bank and card feeds">
    Connect bank and card accounts when current balance observations are useful. These feeds complement the accounting source; they do not replace the books.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create reusable metrics">
    Start with the measures needed for one decision. Use connected system references, saved metrics, reusable inputs, forecast assumptions, and formulas. Validate each metric before using it downstream.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose a Page">
    Bind the metrics to a focused P&L, forecast, cash flow model, KPI view, or management dashboard. Keep the metric definition shared and presentation choices local to each Page block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an agent workflow">
    Configure a reusable workflow, run it manually, review its evidence and output, then enable a schedule and destination. External delivery follows the approval and dispatch policy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first useful milestone

Do not try to model the entire company at once. A strong first milestone is:

1. one ready ledger source,
2. complete high-level Context,
3. five to ten clearly defined metrics,
4. one decision-focused Page, and
5. one manually tested reporting agent.

Then build the flagship recurring workflow: a scheduled CFO brief that reads the client's current finance evidence, drafts the agreed format, and proposes the exact Slack message for human approval.

## Readiness checklist

- The correct client and entity are selected.
- The ledger domain is **ready**, not merely connected.
- Every displayed number has a source period and understandable lineage.
- Missing data is not silently converted to zero.
- Inputs and assumptions have owners and review dates.
- The agent's destination and approval policy are explicit.
- A manual test has been reviewed before scheduling.
