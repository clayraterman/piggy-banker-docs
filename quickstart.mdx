Build one useful workflow from connected books to a reviewed result. Use a clearly labeled test client or non-sensitive example when exploring sharing and automation for the first time.

## Before you begin

- You can authorize the intended accounting company and read its reports.
- You know the entity, currency, accounting basis, and completed month you will compare.
- You have permission to edit the workspace. An account owner may need to complete billing before adding a managed client or enabling paid work.

You can explore Pages with manual inputs before connecting a ledger. Label those inputs as assumptions; they are not accounting actuals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the right workspace">
    Sign in at [finance.piggybanker.io](https://finance.piggybanker.io). Company accounts enter their own workspace. CFO Firms start in Mission Control and open a managed client; use the account's Settings flow to add the first client.

    Confirm the business name before authorizing a source or entering data. See [billing and usage](/reference/billing) if client creation requires billing setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and inspect one accounting source">
    Open **Connections**, choose the provider shown for your workspace, and authorize the correct company. Complete any data-selection step and wait for the initial sync.

    Open **Source data** from Connections, or open **Pages** → **Metrics** → **Source data**. Inspect the dataset and date coverage. Authorization alone does not prove that the values you need have arrived.

    **Checkpoint:** Find one known amount for the same entity, period, basis, and currency as its provider report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the business explanation">
    In **Context**, review **Company**, **Money**, and **Strategy**. Record the operating model, fiscal conventions, key exclusions, and the decision this workflow should support. Keep source amounts in Metrics or source data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your first metrics">
    In **Pages**, open **Metrics** and choose **New metric**, or add a metric from the relevant source data. Give it a clear name, source, number format, and rollup. Use a formula or manual input only when that is the intended definition.

    Start with a small set you can reconcile. An ending balance generally needs a different rollup from monthly revenue.

    **Checkpoint:** Close and reopen Metrics, then confirm that the definition and sample value remain correct. See [Create a metric](/setup/create-metrics).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Page and reload it">
    Use **New page** in the sidebar, name the Page, and add a metric-backed block. Choose the saved metric, period, and scenario. Add a short note explaining the source and the question the Page answers.

    Wait for **Saved**, leave the Page, and return. Confirm that the title, notes, metric selection, and value persist. A missing value needs investigation; it is not a zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test an agent with the same inputs">
    Open **Agents**, choose or create an agent, and review its instructions, Skills, sources, and model. Allow only the inputs needed for this workflow. Keep scheduling and external delivery off during the first test.

    A test can consume model usage. Check your account's usage settings before running it. Review the completed result against the same saved metrics and Page; do not accept a plausible explanation with the wrong period or source.

    **Checkpoint:** The run completes, its output remains available after reload, and you can trace its important claims to the selected evidence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make it repeatable

Follow [Verify your first workflow](/guides/verify-your-first-workflow) to check five values, repeat sync, and agent grounding. Only then choose a schedule or destination. Review the exact recipient and content before approving external delivery.

[Sharing and access](/reference/sharing-and-access) explains how a workspace link, secret Page link, PDF, and agent delivery differ.
